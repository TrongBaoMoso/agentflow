# P4 S8: the HR hand-off is the single "joined" path

Bead: `agentflow-0gwad.5.9` (parent `agentflow-0gwad.5`). Plan row: `P4-PLAN.md` §3, S8. Date: 08/11/2026.

## 1. What changes, in one paragraph

When a Loan Officer joins, Recruit already sends HR a `recruit.hired.v1` hand-off (D143, live in prod since 07/10), and HR auto-creates the associate (ai-hr-be `IntakeRecruitHire` → `hireRecruitDraft`). S8 adds the three things HR still asks for by hand: whether the LO is **W-2 or 1099**, the **experience class** (which picks the commission plan accounting reads from HR), and the **signed agreement PDFs** (document-esign DMS file ids, from P4 S5). HR then files the agreement as the hire's `us_lo_agreement` document by copying it out of DMS, instead of someone uploading it.

## 2. The contract (recruit-be D406)

Additive keys on the same `contract_version: "recruit.hired.v1"`, sent only while `recruit.features.hr-handoff-agreement-fields` is on (staging: on; production: absent = off). Every key is omitted when Recruit does not hold the fact (never `null`, never `[]`). The full key list is pinned in `recruit-be/src/test/resources/contracts/recruit.hired.v1.json` (`RecruitHiredV1ContractTest`); renaming, retyping or removing a key needs `recruit.hired.v2`.

| Key | Type / values | Source in Recruit | HR use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `contract_type` | `W2` \| `1099` | 1-1 pick `lo_type` (OUTSIDE = W2, INDEPENDENT = 1099), else `employment_type` (`contract` = 1099; `full_time`/`part_time`/`outside_sales_person` = W2; anything else omitted) | Informational. HR already derives it from `employment_type` + `lo_type` |
| `experience_level` | `NEWLY_LICENSED` \| `INEXPERIENCED` \| `EXPERIENCED` \| `HIGH_PRODUCER` | D302 decided level, else MOSO `experience` (Newly_licensed / New / Experienced / Team_lead); UNKNOWN omitted | Informational (finer than HR's two values) |
| `experience_class` | `inexperienced` \| `experienced` | NEWLY_LICENSED + INEXPERIENCED → inexperienced; EXPERIENCED + HIGH_PRODUCER → experienced | `EmployeeCreate.ExperienceLevel` → commission plan seeding, packs `experience` |
| `signed_agreements` | `[{doc_type, dms_file_id, signed_at}]` | every COMPLETED `agreement_envelopes` row of the joined offer (`signed_files`, D409, PR #906), united and de-duplicated by file id; `signed_at` = that envelope's completion. Absent for a MOSO-signed LO and while `esign-de` is off | Copied out of DMS and filed; the W-2/1099 form answers `us_lo_agreement`, addenda are free `contract` papers |
| `agreement_envelope_id` | string | the latest completed document-esign envelope id | Trace only |

Recruit's read of S5's table runs in its own read-only `REQUIRES_NEW` transaction, checks `to_regclass('agreement_envelopes')` first (S5 may merge after S8), and answers any failure as "no files". It can never abort the hand-off tick or dead-letter a hire. A log line per delivery shows only key names and the two enum values (`HR handoff payload for candidate <id>: contract_type=… experience_class=… signed_agreements=N keys=[…]`).

Template codes that count as "the main agreement" (HR side): `1099_agreement_form_finalized`, `w2_agreement_form_finalized`, and the legacy `ilo_1099_agreement`, `olo_w2_agreement`. Everything else (`exclusive_agent_agreement`, `ca_compliance_statement`) is an addendum.

## 5. ai-hr-be's own MOSO dependency (not Recruit's)

Measured on ai-hr-be `origin/master` f69a3b89 (08/11):
- On every hire (any path: `POST /employees`, a draft HR submits, the recruit auto-hire), `hireEmployee` writes a `packs_push` outbox row in the hire transaction (`internal/db/draft.go` `enqueueHirePacksPush`) when `PacksPush.PushEnabled()`; after commit `KickPacksPush()` drains it.
- The drain calls packs `execute/register.InviteAdminsOp` (`internal/packsclient/client.go` `CreateAdmin`, header `x-api-key`), which **creates the MOSO Admin** for the new hire: email, role (HR job role → MOSO AdminRole name), names, phone, title, company email, and the `DueAtHire` facts (lo_type, `experience` New/Experienced, start date, branch, bonus level, compensation flags…; `internal/packs/write.go`).
- Gated by `PACKS_PUSH_ENABLED=1` + `PACKS_API_BASE_URL`/`PACKS_API_KEY` + a packs sync source (`wiring_packs.go`). Retries up to 20 attempts, alert after 3, "already exists" → read back and `UpdateMemberOp`.
- So: **as long as MOSO runs, a hire made from Recruit still gets a MOSO Admin record through HR**, and that is ai-hr-be's choice and code, not Recruit's. When MOSO is switched off (P6), HR must turn `PACKS_PUSH_ENABLED` off; nothing in Recruit changes. `experience_class` from S8 also improves what HR pushes to MOSO (`experience`) until then.

## 6. Open questions (with recommendation)

1. **Drafts HR finishes by hand.** ai-hr-be files the agreements only on the auto-hire path; a held draft (rehire, no free company address, a refused create) gets a note "agreement signed in Recruit (N file(s) in DMS)" but the files are not stored on the draft. *Recommendation:* accept for now (auto-hire is the common path); ai-hr-be follow-up: a small side table keyed by draft id that the HR submit path drains.
2. **DMS cross-service read.** The signed files are DMS files owned by document-esign under service type RECRUIT. S5 reports HR reading them needs the RECRUIT caller (document-esign #138) or a download done by recruit-be. *Recommendation:* Khai confirms whether HRIS may `GET /files/{id}/content` on a RECRUIT file; if not, document-esign shares the signed file to HUMAN_RESOURCE on completion (one call). Recruit downloading and re-posting PDFs to HR is the fallback, not the plan.
3. **HIGH_PRODUCER / Team lead → "experienced".** HR has only two classes. *Recommendation:* keep; HR's bonus level, not the class, is where a high producer differs. Ask accounting to confirm Team-lead LOs belong on the Experienced plans.
4. **`contract_type` is redundant** with `employment_type` + `lo_type`. *Recommendation:* keep it (cheap, unambiguous for humans reading a captured request); HR does not need to read it.
