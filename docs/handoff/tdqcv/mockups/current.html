Before you begin

Welcome to Loan Factory! Before you fill out your information, let's make sure we are a good match. Every Loan Officer needs to meet the points below.

Licensing and NMLS

Active status: you need a valid NMLS ID. The states you want us to sponsor must be Active or Approved-Inactive.

Expired license: renew it on NMLS before we can send the sponsorship request.

Pending sponsorship: if you just applied for a state license and only need a sponsoring company, we can sponsor you.

Comfortable with technology

We are a tech-first company. We always show you our own tools, but you need to be comfortable using modern software on your own to manage your pipeline.

Minimum production

At least 1 loan every 6 months to keep your sponsorship active.

Drive and a will to learn

You go after new clients, build your own brand with our tools, stay open to feedback, and are ready to learn new features.
