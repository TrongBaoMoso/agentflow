Compensation

What do these do?

Main target compensation is built into borrower-paid compensation on your website rates, and is the default when you convert a loan. You can raise or lower it per loan when the lender allows.

Correspondent target compensation is required for our correspondent investors. You do not have to choose between wholesale and correspondent; loans can go through either. See the Correspondent Handbook.
