Here are short answers to the questions Loan Officers ask us most.

Can I move my license to Loan Factory?
Yes. We sponsor your NMLS license. Your state license should be Active or Approved-Inactive. If it expired, renew it in NMLS first. Approval usually takes a few days.

How am I paid?
On 1099, you keep 100% of the commission, minus a $595 admin fee and a $500 processing fee per closed loan. On W-2, you get 90% after those fees. Loan Officers are commission-only, so there are no health, 401k or paid time off benefits.

Can I work from home?
Yes, in most states. It depends on each state's rules.

What help do I get?
Live support 7 days a week and ongoing training. New Loan Officers can get a coach, or join a team led by an experienced Team Leader.

Who can join?
Newly licensed Loan Officers, and active producers with 2 or more loans closed in the past 12 months. A background check and management approval are required.
