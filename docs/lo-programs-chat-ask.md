## A. To the space (asking people to reach out to LOs)

> understand, and to get feedback from a couple of loan officers plus Harry (his email
> below).
>
> https://www.loanfactory.com/ambassador-program
> https://www.loanfactory.com/lo-recruiter-program
>
> Could you please help me get these in front of any loan officer you can reach? Anyone
> is fine. One thing that would help me a lot: don't explain anything first — just send
> the link and let them read it once. I want to see what the page does on its own.
>
> Then either forward these questions or ask them on a call:
> 1. In one sentence, what is this program?
> 2. Is it for you? Why or why not?
> 3. What would you have to do to get money out of it?
> 4. How much could you make, and when would it arrive?
> 5. What's the first thing you'd do after reading it?
> 6. Anything you had to read twice?
>
> And if they have time for one more — show them the other page and ask what the
> difference between the two programs is. That's what Thuan is really asking.
>
> Whatever they say, please send it to me raw, even the unflattering parts. That's the
> only way I can tell whether this version works. Thank you!

## One caveat on pasting Thuan's email

Fine for the helpers — it explains why we're asking. But do **not** put it in front of
the loan officer who is going to read the pages. "It is still difficult to understand"
tells them the answer before they look, and their feedback stops being a reading test.
Send them the links bare; the thread can come afterwards.
