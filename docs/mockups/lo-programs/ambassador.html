How it works

Three levels.
Pick where you start.

Everyone can join at Level 1 today. Levels 2 and 3 are approved by leadership and come with a real budget attached — and a real activity standard to keep it.

Level 1

General
Participation

Open to every Loan Factory loan officer and staff member in good standing. No approval needed — turn on the Recruiting tab and you're in.

Monthly marketing budget

$0 no budget at this level

What's required

  • Remain in good standing
  • Turn on the Recruiting tab
  • Use approved recruiting content only
  • Submit interested loan officers through the referral process

What you get

  • Standard Loan Factory referral bonus
  • Approved recruiting content posted on your behalf
To stay active Keep the Recruiting tab on, keep using approved content, keep following the referral process.

Level 2

loan officer
Ambassador

Approved ambassadors active in recruiting outreach, posting, webinar support and loan-officer-to-loan-officer conversations.

Monthly marketing budget

$500 up to, when approved

Who qualifies

  • Active loan officer in good standing
  • 1,000+ professional and social connections preferred
  • Able to represent Loan Factory professionally
  • Can explain our platform, pricing, technology and support
  • Approved by leadership or the program manager

What you get

  • Everything in Level 1
  • Up to $500 / month for approved recruiting marketing
  • MMI reimbursement and support when approved
  • Approved content and campaign support
Every 90 days 3 recruited or onboarded loan officers — or a clear qualified pipeline showing real progress.

Level 3

Senior loan officer
Ambassador

Senior ambassadors producing strong recruiting activity who can carry high-value prospects, webinars and events through to conversion.

Monthly marketing budget

$1,000 up to, when approved

Who qualifies

  • Meets every Level 2 requirement
  • 2,000+ professional and social connections preferred
  • Strong professional presence
  • Demonstrated recruiting activity and professionalism
  • Can support loan officer, Team Leader and branch-level conversations

What you get

  • Everything in Level 2
  • Up to $1,000 / month for approved recruiting marketing
  • Advanced tool support, MMI and LinkedIn support when approved
  • Warm lead and competitor top-producer lists when approved
  • Webinar hosting and co-hosting opportunities
  • Event and conference support · priority campaign support
Every 90 days 6+ recruited or onboarded loan officers — or a strong qualified producer / team pipeline, plus continued webinar, event and warm-pipeline support.

Team Leaders & Corporate Coaches

Recruit for
Loan Factory.
Grow your own
team too.

Team Leaders and Corporate Coaches are strongly encouraged to participate — you can recruit to Loan Factory while building your own team at the same time.

Team Leader status doesn't automatically qualify you. Level 2 and Level 3 still require approval, and Ambassador standards still apply.
For approved Team Leader Ambassadors, we market your team. Team-focused recruiting content · Team Leader spotlight content · approved team posts · help attracting loan officers who fit your team · support recruiting newer and developing loan officers where it makes sense.
