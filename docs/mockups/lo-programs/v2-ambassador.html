The whole program

Three things.
That is all it is.

Turn it on

Open ALLY — the Loan Factory back office you already sign into — and switch Recruiting on. You are now in the program. No form. No approval. No waiting.

Let us post for you

We write the recruiting posts and put them on your social accounts for you. You do not have to write anything, design anything, or think of what to say.

Send us the names

When a loan officer is interested, submit them through the referral form. If they join Loan Factory, you get the referral bonus — the standard company one.

Every loan officer who joins because of you pays you a referral bonus. That part is the same at every level.

The three levels below are not about the bonus. They are about one thing only: how much money Loan Factory puts behind your marketing.

Level 2 — Loan Officer Ambassador

What leadership looks for

  • An active loan officer in good standing.
  • Around 1,000 or more professional and social connections.
  • Someone who represents Loan Factory well in public.
  • Someone who can explain our platform, our pricing, our technology and our support without help.

Level 3 — Senior Loan Officer Ambassador

Everything above, plus

  • Around 2,000 or more connections.
  • A track record of actually recruiting, not just being willing to.
  • Someone comfortable in a conversation with a branch manager or a team leader, not only with an individual loan officer.

Team Leaders and Corporate Coaches should be in this program. The loan officers you bring in can join your team — you are recruiting for the company and for yourself in the same conversation.

In case we used a word you do not use

What these words mean here.

ALLY

The Loan Factory back office you already sign into. The Recruiting switch lives in there.

Referral bonus

The standard bonus Loan Factory already pays anyone here who brings in a loan officer who joins. This program does not change the amount.

Marketing budget

Company money for recruiting ads, content and tools. It is spent on recruiting, and it is approved before it is spent. It is not cash paid to you.

MMI

A paid data service that shows which loan officers are closing how much, so you know who is worth calling. At Level 2 and 3 we pay for your subscription.

In good standing

You are active at Loan Factory with no open compliance or performance issues.

Hired in 90 days

A loan officer you referred who signed and is working at Loan Factory — not just someone you talked to. This is what keeps a Level 2 or Level 3 budget.
