The one rule to get right

Not every loan officer counts.

A hire only counts if that loan officer closed 12 or more loans in their last 12 months.

The 12 loans are theirs, not yours. You are not required to have closed anything. Your job is to find people who already close.

What it pays

Add up what your hires closed last year. That is your bonus.

At the end of each recruiting month we add up the loans every one of your qualifying hires closed in their previous 12 months. Every 12 loans in that total is $1,000 to you, paid once, up to $30,000.

How the work actually goes

Find them. Talk to them. Stay with them.

  1. Find them and write them down

    Spot the loan officer, branch or team, then register them in the CRM straight away. Whoever writes a candidate down first owns that candidate.

  2. Have the first conversation

    Talk to them properly. If it helps, walk them through Loan Factory and TERA one-to-one.

  3. Get them on the webinar

    Sign them up for the weekly Loan Factory webinar, so they hear it from leadership and ask their own questions live instead of taking your word for it.

  4. Answer the hard questions and close

    Deal with the real objections — pricing, support, what moving actually costs them — and help them decide.

  5. Stay until they are funding

    Signing is not the finish line. You stay alongside the onboarding team until that loan officer is actually closing loans at Loan Factory.

In case we used a word you do not use

What these words mean here.

Unit

One closed loan. "12 units" and "12 closed loans" are the same thing — we say closed loans on this page.

Verified

We checked their production against public data before it counted. Their word for it is not enough.

Qualifying hire

A loan officer, branch or team you brought in who closed 12 or more loans in their last 12 months. Only these count toward your month and your bonus.

Last 12 months

A rolling year counted backwards from today — not January to December.

Recruiting month

The month your hires are counted in. It closes at month end, and after that the total does not move — that is what your bonus is calculated on.

One-time bonus

Paid once for that month's hires. It is not a salary and it is not paid again every month for the same person.
