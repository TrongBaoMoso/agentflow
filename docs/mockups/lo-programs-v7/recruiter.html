The recruiting process

Five steps,
start to active.

  1. Source and register

    Identify the loan officer, branch or team, and register the candidate in the CRM and referral system. First documented owner keeps the candidate.

  2. First conversation

    Hold the initial conversation and, where it helps, give a one-to-one tour of Loan Factory and TERA.

  3. Get them on the webinar

    Register the prospect for the weekly Loan Factory webinar so they hear directly from leadership and can ask their own questions live.

  4. Follow up and close

    Address concerns, answer the hard questions, and help the candidate reach a decision.

  5. Stay through activation

    Support the Onboarding team until the loan officer is fully active at Loan Factory. The handoff isn't the finish line.
