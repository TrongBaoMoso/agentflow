How it works

Three Levels
of Participation

Eligible LOs and staff can participate at Level 1 by enabling Recruiting in ALLY. Levels 2 and 3 require leadership/program manager approval and come with a monthly marketing budget — and an activity target to keep it.

Level 1

General
Participation

Open to all LOs and staff in good standing. Turn on Recruiting in ALLY to participate.

Monthly marketing budget

None no monthly marketing budget

What's required

  • Remain in good standing
  • Turn on Recruiting in ALLY
  • Use approved recruiting content only
  • Submit interested loan officers through the referral process

What you get

  • Standard Loan Factory referral bonus
  • Approved recruiting content posted on your behalf
To stay active Keep Recruiting on in ALLY, keep using approved content, keep following the referral process.

Level 2

Loan Officer
Ambassador

Approved Ambassadors who actively participate in recruiting outreach, content, webinar support, and LO-to-LO conversations.

Monthly marketing budget

Up to $500 per month

Who qualifies

  • Active loan officer in good standing
  • 1,000+ professional and social connections preferred
  • Able to represent Loan Factory professionally
  • Can explain our platform, pricing, technology and support
  • Approved by leadership or the program manager

What you get

  • Everything in Level 1
  • Up to $500 / month for approved recruiting marketing
  • MMI reimbursement and support when approved
  • Approved content and campaign support
90-Day Activity Target 3 recruited/onboarded LOs over 90 days, or a qualified pipeline showing meaningful progress.

Level 3

Senior Loan Officer
Ambassador

Approved Senior Ambassadors with strong recruiting activity who can support higher-value prospects, webinars, events, and recruiting conversations.

Monthly marketing budget

Up to $1,000 per month

Who qualifies

  • Meets every Level 2 requirement
  • 2,000+ professional and social connections preferred
  • Strong professional presence
  • Demonstrated recruiting activity and professionalism
  • Can support loan officer, Team Leader and branch-level conversations

What you get

  • Everything in Level 2
  • Up to $1,000 / month for approved recruiting marketing
  • Advanced tool support, MMI and LinkedIn support when approved
  • Warm lead and competitor top-producer lists when approved
  • Webinar hosting and co-hosting opportunities
  • Event and conference support · priority campaign support
90-Day Activity Target 6+ recruited/onboarded LOs over 90 days, or a strong qualified producer/team pipeline with continued recruiting support.

Team Leaders & Corporate Coaches

Recruit for
Loan Factory.
Grow your own
team too.

Team Leaders and Corporate Coaches are strongly encouraged to participate — you can recruit to Loan Factory while building your own team at the same time.

Team Leader status doesn't automatically qualify you. Level 2 and Level 3 still require approval, and Ambassador standards still apply.
For approved Team Leader Ambassadors, Loan Factory can help market your team for recruiting. Team-focused recruiting content · Team Leader spotlight content · approved team posts · help attracting loan officers who fit your team · support recruiting newer and developing loan officers where it makes sense.
