Notes for review

What to look at

Six auth states, one URL

Public · not applied · under review · approved · not approved · admin. The Preview as bar is a presenter tool, not part of the design — it comes out before implementation.

Real brand tokens

Orange #f36f20, ink #12151c, Montserrat — taken from themeConfigVariables.ts, so this maps straight onto Mantine + Tailwind.

Roster uses real people

Photos, names and NMLS numbers come from the public Loan Factory directory. No search or filter — grouped by level, with the level on each card. The level against each person is illustrative only, not a real assignment.

Status is in the hero

Your application status now sits in the first screen, in the hero rail — no scrolling to the foot of the page to find out where you stand.

Admin sees numbers, not the pitch

Signed in as an admin, the section under the hero becomes registration counts, a level split and the review queue. Queue names are invented.

Registering is a modal

The CTA is in the hero on every page and state, and opens the form as a popup. Fields pair up two and three across instead of one per row.

Bonus table is real HTML now

All 30 tiers from 12+ units to 360+ units, rebuilt from the PNG. Responsive, and it collapses behind a disclosure on phones instead of scrolling sideways.

Internal material left out

No base salary figures, no termination language, no internal staff names, no CRM ownership rules, no call-tracking requirement. Say the word if any of it should go back in.

Out of scope, per Phuong

The Immediate Launch Items list is not on these pages — including the unique recruiting link / QR code and the ALLY Recruiting tab work.
