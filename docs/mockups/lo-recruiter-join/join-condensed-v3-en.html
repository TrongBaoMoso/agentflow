Straight answers

The three we get most

Does registering for the webinar commit me to anything?

No. It books you a seat and sends the joining link. There is no fee, no agreement and no credit or licence check at that stage.

When am I charged?

Only at the startup-fee step of the full application, and only after you have chosen to go ahead. The amount is the same for everyone — no special deals, no fine print.

Do I have to move all my state licences at once?

No. The licensing team works through them with you, and you can start originating in the states that transfer first.
