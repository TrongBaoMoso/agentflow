Before you ask me

The five questions I get every week.

How long does the license sponsorship take?

It starts the day you give us NMLS access, and the timeline is the state's, not ours. I'll tell you what to expect for your specific states on our call rather than quote you an average that may not apply.

What does "100% commission" really mean?

You keep the commission on the file, minus a flat $595 per file, and $500 for in-house processing on a closed loan. There is no split, no tier, and no annual renegotiation.

Is the $100 startup fee the only cost?

It is the only fee to join, and it is one time. There is no desk fee, no monthly fee and no software subscription — the platform is built in-house and is free to you.

What happens to the loans I have in process?

They stay where they are until they close. We plan the timing of your move around them, which is most of what my first call with you is about.

I'm not licensed yet. Should I still send this?

Yes. Newly licensed officers are eligible. Leave the NMLS field blank and I'll tell you the order to do things in.

For the reviewers, not for the visitor

What this page is, in three sentences.

One page per recruiter. The URL already knows who sent the visitor, so the "who told you about Loan Factory" field is filled in and locked — the candidate cannot change it and the recruiter cannot lose the credit. Everything else on the page is the copy we already publish on /loan-officer.

The link is the identity

Two links, one page

/join/seth-august is the one he posts on Facebook — six questions, no fee. /join/seth-august/apply is the one he emails a professional he has already spoken to — the full application including the $100 step. Different links so he never sends the wrong one, and so the two funnels can be measured apart.
