Who this team recruits

✓ Qualifies

Loan officers with at least 12 verified funded units in the preceding 12 months.

Branches and teams qualify on the same basis.

– Does not count toward this program

Newly licensed Loan Officers and anyone below 12 verified units.

They're routed to our existing onboarding team — still welcome at Loan Factory, just not part of this program's scope.

The roles

The team starts with one Team Lead and three to five Recruiters, and operates separately from the Loan Officer Ambassador Program and the existing onboarding team.

Tier 01

Strategic support

Sets program direction, steps in on major recruiting opportunities and problem-solving.

Tier 02

Recruiting Team Lead

Runs the team day to day, recruits personally, coaches recruiters, reviews pipelines, reports results.

Tier 03 · hiring

Producing Loan Officer Recruiters

Source, educate and convert qualified producers, then stay involved through onboarding and activation.
