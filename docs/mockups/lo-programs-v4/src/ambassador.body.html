Section 01

The three
levels

Every level is real participation with real support behind it. Level 1 is self-serve and open today. Levels 2 and 3 are approved roles with a company-funded marketing budget and a 90-day activity standard.

Who
Every Loan Factory loan officer or staff member in good standing. No approval needed.
To qualify
Turn on the Recruiting tab. Use approved content only. Submit interested loan officers through the referral process. Stay in good standing.
To stay active · per 90 days
Keep the Recruiting tab on, keep using approved content, keep following the referral process.
What you get
·Standard Loan Factory referral bonus ·Approved recruiting content posted on your behalf
Who
Approved ambassadors active in recruiting outreach, posting, webinar support and conversations with other loan officers.
To qualify
·Active loan officer in good standing ·1,000+ professional connections preferred ·Able to represent Loan Factory professionally ·Able to explain our platform, pricing, technology and support ·Approved by leadership
To stay active · per 90 days
3 recruited or onboarded loan officers, or a clear qualified pipeline showing real progress.
What you get
·Everything in Level 1 ·Up to $500/mo marketing budget ·MMI reimbursement when approved ·Campaign support
Who
Approved senior ambassadors producing strong recruiting activity, supporting high-value prospects, webinars and events.
To qualify
·Meets all Level 2 requirements ·2,000+ professional connections preferred ·Strong professional presence ·Demonstrated recruiting activity ·Able to support loan officer, Team Leader and branch-level conversations ·Approved by leadership
To stay active · per 90 days
6+ recruited or onboarded loan officers, or a strong qualified producer / team pipeline, plus continued support on webinars, events and warm pipeline follow-up.
What you get
·Everything in Level 2 ·Up to $1,000/mo marketing budget ·Advanced tool support · MMI / LinkedIn / recruiting tools ·Warm lead and competitor top-producer lists when approved ·Webinar hosting and co-hosting ·Event and conference support ·Priority campaign support
