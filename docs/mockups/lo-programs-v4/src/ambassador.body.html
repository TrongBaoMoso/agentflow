The three
levels

Every level is a real seat in the program. Level 1 needs no approval. Levels 2 and 3 add budget, tools and support in exchange for measured recruiting activity.

Also for leadership

Team Leaders and Corporate Coaches are strongly encouraged to participate.

Team Leaders make valuable ambassadors — they can recruit to Loan Factory while growing their own teams.

Team Leader status does not automatically qualify you for Level 2 or Level 3. Approval is still required and Ambassador standards still apply.

For approved Team Leader Ambassadors, Loan Factory supports your team's recruiting with team-focused recruiting content, Team Leader spotlight content, approved team posts, and help attracting loan officers who fit your team.
