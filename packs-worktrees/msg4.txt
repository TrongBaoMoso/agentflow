fix(recruiting): program credit holds only while the lead still belongs to the credited member

The marker alone went stale when staff reassigned a marked lead (AssignOwners,
Recruit sync, CSV). A lead is now member credit only when the marker is set AND
recruiter equals lead_origin_owner_key (both written at create). One definition,
LoRecruiterProgramStanding.isMemberCredit, is used by the staff-automation
guards and by both reports' non-staff admission, so a reassigned lead returns
to staff handling and the new recruiter does not inherit the credit.
