fix(recruiting): server-only credit fields, stale-cache handling, dual role, bonus hold (review round)

M1 the credit fields are server-only: beforeSave rolls back any update that
changes them (a server write is marked by LoRecruiterProgramStanding.confirm /
reconcile). M2 public bodies lose recruiter again.
- cache: a not-a-member answer from a copy older than 60 s forces one refresh; a
  copy older than 1 h is UNVERIFIED; single-flight stale-while-revalidate (only the
  first load blocks); lenient list parsing (skip and count malformed spells);
  repeated refresh failures log a distinct WARN; 4 MB body cap.
- a signed-in Loan Officer's own lead is flagged unverified when no list exists.
- reconcile also re-checks the last 48 h of referral leads whose referrer has any
  membership spell, at the lead's creation time.
- a staff recruiter who is also a member never gets the program marker.
- the LO-referral bonus is also held while a lead is unverified; reconcile logs a
  clear note when it settles as not credited after the lead was converted.
- monthly cron recomputes the credited leads once per run; the CSV import logs row
  numbers instead of row contents.
