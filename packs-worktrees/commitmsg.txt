feat(recruiting): credit approved LO Recruiter Program members as recruiter

A sign-up through the own site of a Loan Officer who is an approved LO
Recruiter Program member but has no HR recruiter flag was stored as
referred_source=loan_officer + referred_lo. Accounting pays the program
from MOSO, so store it exactly as an HR-flagged recruiter's lead:
referred_source=recruiter, recruiter=<owner key name>, no referred_lo.

Both proofs are required: the server-resolved slug stamp (SLUG_SERVER_RESOLVED
naming the same Admin) and moso-aid's public GET /api/join-page?key=<websafe
Admin key> returning data.recruiter_slug equal to the stamp slug. Anything
short of a clear "member" (property unset, timeout, non-2xx, bad body, other
slug) keeps today's loan_officer credit and logs. Evaluated only in
LORecruiting.beforeCreated, so existing leads are never re-credited.

New config: system property moso_aid_base_url (moso-aid /api root); unset
means the feature is off.
